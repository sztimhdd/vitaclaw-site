# Feature Research: VitaClaw Website OmniGraph Integration

**Source basis:** Local repo instructions and orchestrator handoff documents, not external research.
**Date:** 2026-05-08

## Table Stakes For Product Phase 1

### Agent 技术动态

- Early homepage section.
- Exactly 5 curated items.
- Each item includes original title, original URL, Chinese SEO-friendly summary, and tags.
- Titles open the original source URL.
- Light caveat: summaries are automatic and source articles remain authoritative.

### VitaClaw 助手

- Right-bottom floating entry point.
- Public-facing name is `VitaClaw 助手`.
- Presales scenario consultant role.
- FAQ buttons for approval, data entry, checking/reconciliation, backfill, audit, and cross-system automation.
- Static or locally bounded responses in Phase 1.
- Soft lead capture options that do not gate conversation.

## Differentiators To Defer Until Inputs Exist

- Real document RAG for product-specific assistant answers.
- Daily OmniGraph export automation after export shape is confirmed.
- Deep research reports with citations.

## Anti-Features

- Phase 1 knowledge-base app.
- Heavy enterprise gates.
- Internal article detail pages.
- Premature data pipeline architecture.
- Publicly exposing Hermes as the assistant brand.

## Confidence

High. Feature scope is explicitly locked in the handoff.
